Common questions

Can't find your answer? Call us on 8083980227.

How do I book an appointment?

Tap any “Book appointment” button on this page and fill in the short form, or call 8083980227, 9708380543 or 9162732404. Our staff will call you back to confirm your appointment time.

Is the Friday consultation really free?

Yes. Every Friday, patients are seen by the doctor free of charge. Friday OPD timing: aaaaaaaaaaaaaaa. Medicines and tests, if needed, are charged separately.

Are emergency services available at night?

Yes, emergency services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. In an emergency, call any of our numbers and come straight to the hospital. You do not need an appointment.

Can I walk in without an appointment?

You can, but booking ahead reduces your waiting time because the staff can give you a time slot. Walk-in patients are seen in order of arrival: aaaaaaaaaaaaaaa.

Which illnesses does the doctor treat?

Dr. Hussain treats sugar (diabetes), BP, thyroid, stomach, chest, heart and urinary problems, along with everyday illnesses like fever, infections and weakness. If you need a specialist, he will examine you first and guide you to the right one.

What should I bring to my visit?

Bring your old prescriptions, any recent test reports and a list of the medicines you are taking now. For diabetes or BP patients, a record of your recent sugar or BP readings is very helpful.

Do I need to come fasting for a sugar test?

For a fasting blood sugar test you usually need 8 to 10 hours without food. Plain water is fine. Ask our staff when you book, and don't stop any regular medicine unless the doctor tells you to.

Are lab tests and ECG available at the hospital?

aaaaaaaaaaaaaaa. If a test is needed, the doctor will tell you during your consultation and our staff will guide you on where and when to get it done.

Is a follow-up visit charged again?

Long-term conditions like diabetes, BP and thyroid need regular follow-up to keep them under control. Follow-up fee and how long a consultation stays valid: aaaaaaaaaaaaaaa.

Can I book an appointment for a family member?

Yes. Enter the patient's name and age in the form and give your own mobile number. We will call you to confirm, and the patient should come with you at the booked time.

How do I reach the hospital?

We are at Chakiya Mod, Yaduvanshi Nagar, Siwan. Tap “Get directions” in the location section to open Google Maps, or call us and we'll guide you from the nearest landmark.
